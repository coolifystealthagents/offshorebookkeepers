# Website Optimization Project

## Current remediation scope

This decision record documents the September 16, 2026 Offshore Bookkeepers service gap analysis and whole-site remediation. It records current work only; it does not rewrite the history or evidence state of an earlier optimization run.

## Service gap analysis

The previous catalog covered ten useful scopes but repeated the same tasks, controls, and onboarding plan. It also omitted common VA-performable bookkeeping work involving receipts, cleanup, vendor records, timesheets, cash inputs, audit documents, assets, system migrations, inventory, job costs, and sales-tax data preparation.

The current catalog uses six customer-oriented groups and twenty-one dedicated pages. Every page names a real operating problem, concrete tasks, required inputs, prepared outputs, client-retained controls, a first-week plan, and a relevant local workflow illustration.

### Keep the books current

- `daily-transaction-coding`
- `bank-reconciliation-support`
- `receipt-expense-documentation`
- `bookkeeping-cleanup-catch-up`

### Manage money in and money out

- `accounts-receivable-support`

### Support payroll and workforce records

### Prepare for close and management review

## Claim and control boundaries

The catalog limits the remote role to preparation, organization, reconciliation to supplied records, exception logging, and reviewer handoff. Payment release, accounting-policy decisions, tax positions and filings, legal conclusions, assurance, valuation, management judgment, and final approval remain with the client or qualified adviser.

No testimonial, rating, result, savings, staffing-volume, certification, or customer-logo claim was added. Unsupported testimonial modules and parent-brand promotional sections were removed.

## Conversion and canonical decisions

- `/contact-us` is the sole contact page.
- `/contact` permanently redirects to `/contact-us`.
- The contact experience asks for one real bottleneck and explains source records, access boundaries, reviewer ownership, and a controlled sample.
- Legacy service and article targets permanently redirect, while all first-party links point directly to current canonical destinations.

## Acceptance evidence

The release is accepted only after local contracts pass, the production build succeeds, the exact commit is deployed, and the full production sitemap-plus-internal-link union passes structural and responsive checks at desktop, tablet, and mobile widths.
