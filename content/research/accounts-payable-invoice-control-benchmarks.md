## Key statistic
This report reviewed 10 authoritative public sources and separates sourced control principles from operating recommendations. The count is the evidence-set size, not a claim about market performance.

## What the evidence supports
The strongest AP metric is controlled exception resolution, not raw invoice throughput. Use the 10-source evidence set to define intake, coding, approval, duplicate checks, payment release, and retention as separate control steps.

## Measurement design
Measure invoices received, invoices missing evidence, duplicate candidates, approval aging, and post-payment corrections. Do not turn a count into a quality claim without defining the denominator. A distributed team can prepare coding and evidence while authorized client staff retain payment release and bank administration. Review sampled packets before expanding access.

## Operating boundary
A distributed team can prepare coding and evidence while authorized client staff retain payment release and bank administration. Review sampled packets before expanding access. Keep approval, payment release, legal interpretation, and material accounting judgment with designated owners.

## Methodology and limitations
Method: 10 public sources were compared. They provide control principles rather than a market price or universal error rate. Validate local tax, retention, and payment rules with qualified advisers. Recommendations should be tested against the organization’s systems, contracts, jurisdiction, and risk tolerance.
