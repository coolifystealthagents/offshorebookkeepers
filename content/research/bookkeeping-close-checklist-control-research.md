## Key statistic
This study reviewed 10 authoritative sources; it is an evidence-set count, not a market benchmark.

## What the evidence supports
Control frameworks consistently connect reliable processing to assigned responsibility, evidence, and review. A close checklist should therefore name the preparer, reviewer, input, output, and due date for every material step.

## Checklist design
Group steps into inputs, reconciliations, exception resolution, review, and sign-off. Track completion and exceptions separately so a checked box cannot hide an unresolved item.

## Methodology and limitations
We reviewed 10 public sources on internal control, recordkeeping, security, fraud, and bookkeeping work. Recommendations are operational guidance, not accounting or legal advice.
