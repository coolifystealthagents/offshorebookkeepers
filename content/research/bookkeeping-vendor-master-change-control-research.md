## Key statistic
The control design is grounded in 10 public sources; it is not a fraud-rate estimate.
## Change record
Capture requestor, reason, effective date, old value, new value, approver, verification method, and evidence location.
## Review signals
Review changes near payment runs, duplicate banking details, urgent requests, and edits lacking independent confirmation. Use exception queues rather than informal messages.
## Methodology and limitations
This report synthesizes public control, recordkeeping, security, audit, and labor guidance. It does not prescribe a particular accounting platform.
