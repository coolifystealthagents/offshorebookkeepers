## Key statistic
This report reviewed 10 authoritative public sources and separates sourced control principles from operating recommendations. The count is the evidence-set size, not a claim about market performance.

## What the evidence supports
Subscription schedules need clear source ownership and a repeatable review trail. Build the 10-source evidence set around contracts, billing exports, cancellations, refunds, revenue schedules, journal support, and reviewer sign-off.

## Measurement design
Useful process measures include schedule exceptions, contract-to-billing mismatches, late inputs, and correction journals. These measures do not replace accounting judgment. A support team can prepare schedule inputs and reconcile exports; an appropriately qualified owner should approve recognition policy and material judgments.

## Operating boundary
A support team can prepare schedule inputs and reconcile exports; an appropriately qualified owner should approve recognition policy and material judgments. Keep approval, payment release, legal interpretation, and material accounting judgment with designated owners.

## Methodology and limitations
Method: sources support records, control, audit, access, and labor context. This is not accounting advice and does not prescribe a recognition policy. Recommendations should be tested against the organization’s systems, contracts, jurisdiction, and risk tolerance.
