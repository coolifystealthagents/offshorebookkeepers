## Key statistic
The report uses 10 public sources and does not estimate a market-wide deposit or refund rate.
## Evidence design
Start with cash and receipt evidence, map deposits to customers and obligations, and document the event that releases or refunds the balance.
## Review workflow
Bookkeeping support maintains the schedule and reconciles it to the ledger. An owner reviews aged items, disputes, and policy judgments.
## Limitations
Contract terms and revenue rules vary. Confirm the treatment with the qualified accounting owner.
