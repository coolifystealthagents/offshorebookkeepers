## Key statistic
This report reviewed 10 authoritative public sources and separates sourced control principles from operating recommendations. The count is the evidence-set size, not a claim about market performance.

## What the evidence supports
Ecommerce reconciliation is a mapping problem before it is a volume problem. Use the 10-source evidence set to define order source, settlement report, fee mapping, refund evidence, payout match, and exception owner.

## Measurement design
Measure unmatched payouts, fee-mapping exceptions, refund lag, and aged reconciling items by channel. Keep each channel’s assumptions visible. Distributed support works best when the preparer owns repeatable matching and the finance owner retains judgment over reserves, write-offs, and material adjustments.

## Operating boundary
Distributed support works best when the preparer owns repeatable matching and the finance owner retains judgment over reserves, write-offs, and material adjustments. Keep approval, payment release, legal interpretation, and material accounting judgment with designated owners.

## Methodology and limitations
Method: sources were reviewed for records, controls, security, audit evidence, labor context, and data context. No universal reconciliation rate is asserted. Recommendations should be tested against the organization’s systems, contracts, jurisdiction, and risk tolerance.
