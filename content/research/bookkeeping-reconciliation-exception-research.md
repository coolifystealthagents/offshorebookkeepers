## Key statistic
The research set contains 10 authoritative sources and makes no claim that all organizations share one exception rate.

## What the evidence supports
Internal-control guidance favors timely identification, documented resolution, and management review. An exception log turns reconciliation differences into observable work rather than informal messages.

## Operating model
Use one record per exception, a defined aging threshold, and a reviewer queue. Close an item only when the resolution evidence is attached and the reviewer agrees the balance is explained.

## Methodology and limitations
The review covered public control, recordkeeping, security, fraud, labor, and data sources. Thresholds must be calibrated to transaction volume and materiality.
