## Key statistic
This report reviewed 10 authoritative public sources and separates sourced control principles from operating recommendations. The count is the evidence-set size, not a claim about market performance.

## What the evidence supports
A rent roll is an evidence set, not merely a list of balances. Define the 10-source evidence set around lease data, billed rent, receipts, deposits, adjustments, owner reports, and access controls.

## Measurement design
Track unmatched receipts, lease-to-ledger exceptions, unexplained adjustments, and report review aging. Keep property and entity dimensions explicit. A remote bookkeeper can prepare reconciliations and owner-report support. Authorized managers retain lease decisions, concessions, trust handling, and payment approvals.

## Operating boundary
A remote bookkeeper can prepare reconciliations and owner-report support. Authorized managers retain lease decisions, concessions, trust handling, and payment approvals. Keep approval, payment release, legal interpretation, and material accounting judgment with designated owners.

## Methodology and limitations
Method: public sources cover recordkeeping, controls, audit evidence, security, and workforce context. Local property, tax, and trust requirements need specialist review. Recommendations should be tested against the organization’s systems, contracts, jurisdiction, and risk tolerance.
