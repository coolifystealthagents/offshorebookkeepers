## Key statistic
This report reviewed 10 authoritative public sources and separates sourced control principles from operating recommendations. The count is the evidence-set size, not a claim about market performance.

## What the evidence supports
Trust-account support requires matter-level traceability and a conservative escalation boundary. Define the 10-source evidence set around client ledger, bank activity, matter reference, authorization, reconciliation, and reviewer sign-off.

## Measurement design
Measure unreconciled items, missing matter references, stale balances, and review aging. Do not present these as legal-compliance rates. A support role may organize reconciliations and evidence under written procedures. Attorneys or designated local professionals retain trust decisions, transfers, and compliance interpretation.

## Operating boundary
A support role may organize reconciliations and evidence under written procedures. Attorneys or designated local professionals retain trust decisions, transfers, and compliance interpretation. Keep approval, payment release, legal interpretation, and material accounting judgment with designated owners.

## Methodology and limitations
Method: public sources support recordkeeping, control, access, and audit concepts; bar rules and trust requirements vary by jurisdiction and need legal review. Recommendations should be tested against the organization’s systems, contracts, jurisdiction, and risk tolerance.
