## Research question
Variance review is useful only when the comparison is reproducible. The reviewer should be able to identify the period, ledger population, prior basis, and report version without relying on memory.

## Evidence pattern
Classify the movement as timing, volume, rate, classification, missing entry, or unexplained. Link the explanation to invoices, payroll records, bank activity, contracts, or other source evidence. A plausible story is not a substitute for support.

## Review boundary
Bookkeeping can prepare the bridge, test arithmetic, and queue exceptions. The controller or owner decides whether a correction, reclassification, or business explanation is appropriate and records that decision.

## Methodology and limitations
This research combines recordkeeping, internal-control, reporting, audit, fraud, and cybersecurity guidance. It does not set a universal materiality threshold or replace the entity's accounting policy.
