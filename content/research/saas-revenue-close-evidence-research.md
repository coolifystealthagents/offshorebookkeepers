## Key statistic
The research set contains 10 authoritative sources; it is not a SaaS growth or revenue benchmark.

## What the evidence supports
Revenue close evidence should be complete, traceable, and aligned with the organization’s approved accounting policy. Systems and judgments must be distinguished.

## Schedule design
Tie each schedule line to a source identifier, period, calculation, and reviewer. Reconcile totals to the ledger and document overrides or contract exceptions.

## Methodology and limitations
We reviewed 10 sources on control, audit evidence, recordkeeping, security, fraud, and labor. Accounting conclusions require professional review.
