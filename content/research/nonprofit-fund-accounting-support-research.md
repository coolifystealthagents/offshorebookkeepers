## Key statistic
This report reviewed 10 authoritative public sources and separates sourced control principles from operating recommendations. The count is the evidence-set size, not a claim about market performance.

## What the evidence supports
Fund accounting support depends on preserving purpose and evidence through every handoff. Map the 10-source evidence set to grant or donor restrictions, coding dimensions, supporting documents, reconciliations, and approval evidence.

## Measurement design
Track transactions missing fund dimensions, unsupported allocations, late grant reports, and aged corrections. Never infer compliance from throughput alone. A remote preparer can organize evidence and prepare reconciliations. The nonprofit’s authorized finance and program owners retain restriction judgments and approvals.

## Operating boundary
A remote preparer can organize evidence and prepare reconciliations. The nonprofit’s authorized finance and program owners retain restriction judgments and approvals. Keep approval, payment release, legal interpretation, and material accounting judgment with designated owners.

## Methodology and limitations
Method: sources address documentation, control, audit, security, and workforce context. Grant terms and local reporting rules require a qualified nonprofit adviser. Recommendations should be tested against the organization’s systems, contracts, jurisdiction, and risk tolerance.
