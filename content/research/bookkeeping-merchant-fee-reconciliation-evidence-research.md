## Key statistic
Ten public sources support this workflow; the count is not a processor performance benchmark.
## Reconciliation design
Use a settlement reference and period cutoff. Preserve the processor report, bank evidence, mapping, and reviewer conclusion.
## Methodology and limitations
The sources cover records, controls, security, auditability, and operations. Processor contracts, timing, and fee policies differ.
