## Key statistic
This report uses 10 authoritative sources and does not present an invented cash-application performance average.

## What the evidence supports
Reliable cash application depends on complete records, authorized adjustments, and a review trail. Unapplied cash should remain visible until the reason and disposition are documented.

## Workflow design
Capture receipt date, amount, payer, remittance, proposed match, confidence, and reviewer. Age unresolved receipts and escalate disputed or unusual items.

## Methodology and limitations
The evidence set covers control, security, fraud, recordkeeping, and labor sources. Matching rules depend on bank feeds, ERP configuration, and customer behavior.
