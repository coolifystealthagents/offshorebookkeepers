## Key statistic
This report uses 10 public sources as its evidence set. That is a source-count measure, not a universal forecast-accuracy benchmark.
## Evidence and method
Control sources consistently support dated records, review evidence, access discipline, and clear responsibility. Apply those principles to bank balances, receivables, payables, payroll, and known commitments.
## Operating design
Prepare a versioned forecast, attach source reports, flag stale assumptions, and route material changes to the owner. Keep payment release and financing decisions outside the support role.
## Limitations and next steps
Forecast horizons and definitions vary by business. Pilot the checklist against two cycles, then calibrate exception thresholds. Link each number to a source and reviewer.
