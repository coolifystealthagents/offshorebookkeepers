## Key statistic
The dashboard framework is based on 10 authoritative sources and makes no unsupported claim about industry performance.

## Metric design
Every KPI needs a definition, numerator and denominator where relevant, period, system of record, owner, and known limitation. Keep source extracts with the reporting packet.

## Review rhythm
Review open exceptions, reconciliation age, late inputs, correction journals, and completed reviews. Explain changes rather than optimizing a single number.

## Methodology and limitations
These are internal process measures, not accounting conclusions. The owner should approve definitions, thresholds, and any management use.
