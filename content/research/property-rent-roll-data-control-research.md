## Key statistic
The report reviewed 10 authoritative sources and makes no claim about rental yields or vacancy rates.

## What the evidence supports
Reliable property bookkeeping depends on complete source records, controlled changes, and reconciliations between operational and accounting systems.

## Control design
Version the rent roll, capture effective dates, reconcile billed and collected amounts, and age differences. Restrict personally identifiable information to necessary users.

## Methodology and limitations
Sources address control, security, recordkeeping, fraud, labor, and bookkeeping. Lease and jurisdiction requirements require local review.
