## Key statistic
The evidence set contains 10 public sources and does not establish a universal variance threshold.
## Methodology
Compare approved budget, actual ledger activity, forecast, and prior period using stable definitions. Attach source reports and classify timing, volume, rate, coding, and one-off drivers.
## Review controls
Use a preparer, reviewer, threshold, explanation, owner, and due date for each material variance. Age open explanations and carry forward only with explicit ownership.
## Limitations
Thresholds depend on scale, risk, and reporting purpose. Have the business owner approve definitions before relying on the report.
