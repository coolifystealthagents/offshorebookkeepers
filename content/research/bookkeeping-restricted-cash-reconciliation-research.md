## Key statistic
The research uses a 10-source evidence set across accounting, records, controls, security, and fraud risk.

## Reconciliation design
Maintain separate schedules for restricted and unrestricted activity. Tie each balance to a statement and restriction source, then document releases and reviewer sign-off.

## Exception queue
Flag expired restrictions, unexplained transfers, missing agreements, stale balances, and mismatches between schedules and statements.

## Methodology and limitations
Restrictions and presentation depend on the governing agreement and applicable accounting framework. This report provides a control workflow, not a classification conclusion.
