## Key statistic
Ten public sources support this access-review design; it does not claim a universal access-removal time.
## Methodology
Inventory account, user, role, signatory power, MFA status, last review, owner, and action. Compare access to current responsibilities and approvals.
## Escalation
Bookkeeping support can prepare the inventory and flag mismatches. Bank owners and administrators approve and execute changes; support never changes signatory authority independently.
## Limitations
Bank controls and contractual rules vary. Coordinate changes through the institution’s approved process.
