## Key statistic
This report reviewed 10 authoritative public sources and separates sourced control principles from operating recommendations. The count is the evidence-set size, not a claim about market performance.

## What the evidence supports
Job-costing quality depends on preserving the link between source document, cost code, and approved change. Use the 10-source evidence set to define cost-code ownership, invoice evidence, committed costs, change orders, labor inputs, and review.

## Measurement design
Measure uncoded costs, late commitments, unsupported changes, and correction aging by project. A clean ledger cannot compensate for missing field evidence. Distributed support can prepare coding and exception lists while project and finance owners approve change orders, commitments, and final cost judgments.

## Operating boundary
Distributed support can prepare coding and exception lists while project and finance owners approve change orders, commitments, and final cost judgments. Keep approval, payment release, legal interpretation, and material accounting judgment with designated owners.

## Methodology and limitations
Method: 10 public sources were reviewed for recordkeeping, controls, audit evidence, security, and labor context. Contract and tax treatment must be reviewed locally. Recommendations should be tested against the organization’s systems, contracts, jurisdiction, and risk tolerance.
