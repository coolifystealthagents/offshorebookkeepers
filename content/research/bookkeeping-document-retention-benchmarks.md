## Key statistic
This report reviewed 10 authoritative public sources and separates sourced control principles from operating recommendations. The count is the evidence-set size, not a claim about market performance.

## What the evidence supports
Retention is useful only when documents remain findable, protected, and tied to the transaction. Use the 10-source evidence set to define document classes, retention owner, naming, access, review, and disposition approval.

## Measurement design
Measure missing attachments, failed retrieval tests, over-broad access, and unresolved retention exceptions. Do not invent one retention period for every business. A support team can apply naming and filing rules. The business owner and qualified advisers set legal, tax, and contractual retention requirements.

## Operating boundary
A support team can apply naming and filing rules. The business owner and qualified advisers set legal, tax, and contractual retention requirements. Keep approval, payment release, legal interpretation, and material accounting judgment with designated owners.

## Methodology and limitations
Method: public sources establish records and control considerations but do not replace jurisdiction-specific advice. Test retrieval with representative documents before rollout. Recommendations should be tested against the organization’s systems, contracts, jurisdiction, and risk tolerance.
