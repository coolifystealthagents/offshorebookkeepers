## Research question
Which elements of a remote bookkeeping review note help the next reviewer understand what was checked, what remains uncertain, and who owns the next decision? This matters when a close moves between staffed hours and a reviewer cannot ask an immediate follow-up.

## Evidence scope and method
The method synthesizes public guidance on control activities, evidence, continuity, records, and monitoring. It does not claim that a note format is required by those sources. Sample review notes from one close and classify whether each identifies the account, evidence, question, conclusion, exception owner, and date. Compare notes with later corrections, not only with completion status.

## Analysis for offshore bookkeeping
The preparer should state what was performed and attach the relevant support. The reviewer should state the decision or request further evidence. A note saying “checked” leaves both roles ambiguous. A concise note can be sufficient if it names the exception and resolution. A long note can still fail if it copies a checklist without recording judgment. This is a communication design problem across a distributed team.

## Limitations and conclusion
Notes do not prove that a transaction is correct, and a sample may miss undocumented conversations. Different systems retain different histories. The sources support traceability and monitoring, while the note taxonomy is original analysis. The conclusion is limited: review notes increase handoff clarity when they preserve questions, evidence, decisions, and ownership; they are not a substitute for competent review.
