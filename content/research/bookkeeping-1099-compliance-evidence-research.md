## Key statistic
This framework reviews 10 public sources and is not a filing or classification opinion.
## Evidence packet
Maintain a dated vendor register, source documents, review status, follow-up owner, and exception reason.
## Methodology and limitations
Public recordkeeping, tax-form, control, security, audit, and operations guidance informs the workflow. Requirements vary by facts and jurisdiction.
