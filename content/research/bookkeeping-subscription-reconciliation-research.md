## Key statistic
The framework reviewed 10 public sources and does not estimate average subscription waste.
## Evidence design
Maintain vendor, service, owner, renewal, payment, invoice, and cancellation fields. Reconcile the inventory to recurring ledger activity.
## Review workflow
Bookkeeping support identifies missing invoices and unexpected changes. Owners decide renewals, cancellations, and budget exceptions.
## Limitations
Contract and accounting treatment vary. Confirm materiality and policy with the finance owner.
