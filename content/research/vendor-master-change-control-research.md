## Key statistic
Ten public sources were reviewed; this is the study’s source count, not a vendor-fraud prevalence estimate.

## What the evidence supports
Segregation of duties, authorization, and retained evidence reduce the chance that a data change can directly become an unauthorized payment.

## Control points
Require a ticket or approved form, verify sensitive fields through a trusted channel, limit edit permissions, and review the change report after processing.

## Methodology and limitations
The sources cover internal control, security, fraud, recordkeeping, and bookkeeping context. Controls should be aligned to system capabilities and risk.
