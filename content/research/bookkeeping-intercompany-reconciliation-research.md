## Key statistic
Ten public sources inform this guide; it does not claim an average intercompany mismatch rate.
## Methodology
Use a bilateral schedule, standardized counterparty and period fields, confirmation status, currency treatment, and a dated resolution log.
## Close handoff
Support prepares matches and evidence. The consolidation owner reviews differences, approves eliminations, and retains policy judgment.
## Limitations
Group structures, currencies, and reporting frameworks differ. Tailor the control to the entity map and close calendar.
