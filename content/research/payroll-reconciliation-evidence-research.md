## Key statistic
This report reviewed 10 public sources and does not estimate payroll error rates.

## What the evidence supports
Payroll reconciliation should connect approved inputs to the register, accounting entries, liabilities, and payment evidence. Exceptions need independent review.

## Evidence map
Retain the approved change report, payroll register, journal, funding confirmation, liability report, reconciliation, and sign-off. Restrict sensitive fields to authorized users.

## Methodology and limitations
Sources cover control, security, recordkeeping, fraud, labor, and bookkeeping context. Tax and employment requirements vary by jurisdiction.
