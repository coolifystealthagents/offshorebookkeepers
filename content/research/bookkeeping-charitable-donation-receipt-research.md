## Key statistic
This report uses 10 authoritative sources to define its evidence set and makes no unsupported claim about donation levels or tax outcomes.

## Receipt workflow
Capture receipt date, amount, source, restriction language, deposit, and accounting period. Link the record to the bank reconciliation and preserve the original document.

## Exception review
Queue missing receipts, unclear restrictions, split deposits, duplicate entries, and unexplained transfers. Keep restricted-use balances separately visible where required by policy.

## Methodology and limitations
Donation treatment depends on entity facts, donor communications, and applicable standards or tax rules. This is a bookkeeping handoff framework, not legal or tax advice.
