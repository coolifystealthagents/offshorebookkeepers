## Key statistic
The evidence set contains 10 public references; it does not establish a universal accrual rate or close duration.
## Methodology
We translated recordkeeping, internal-control, audit, and security guidance into a cutoff workflow: identify service period, attach evidence, document estimate, review, post, and track reversal.
## Control checklist
Use a dated open-items list, recurring-vendor schedule, subsequent-invoice review, preparer/reviewer fields, and an exception log. Preserve the support for each material estimate.
## Limitations
Accounting standards and materiality depend on the reporting framework and jurisdiction. Have a qualified owner approve the policy and thresholds.
