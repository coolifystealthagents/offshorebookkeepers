## Key statistic
This report reviewed 10 authoritative public sources and separates sourced control principles from operating recommendations. The count is the evidence-set size, not a claim about market performance.

## What the evidence supports
Consolidation handoffs fail when entity identity and intercompany ownership are implicit. Create the 10-source evidence set around entity chart, account mapping, intercompany balances, eliminations, support schedules, and sign-off.

## Measurement design
Track unreconciled intercompany items, mapping exceptions, late entity submissions, and post-close corrections. Segment every metric by entity and period. A support team can prepare entity schedules and exception packs. The authorized finance owner retains consolidation judgments, eliminations approval, and reporting sign-off.

## Operating boundary
A support team can prepare entity schedules and exception packs. The authorized finance owner retains consolidation judgments, eliminations approval, and reporting sign-off. Keep approval, payment release, legal interpretation, and material accounting judgment with designated owners.

## Methodology and limitations
Method: sources were reviewed for recordkeeping, internal controls, audit evidence, security, and labor context. Reporting frameworks and local statutory needs require qualified review. Recommendations should be tested against the organization’s systems, contracts, jurisdiction, and risk tolerance.
