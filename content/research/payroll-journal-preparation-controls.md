## Key statistic
This report reviewed 10 authoritative public sources and separates sourced control principles from operating recommendations. The count is the evidence-set size, not a claim about market performance.

## What the evidence supports
Payroll journal preparation should be separated from payroll approval and bank release. Map the 10-source evidence set to input ownership, journal preparation, variance review, posting approval, and retention.

## Measurement design
Track missing payroll registers, unexplained variances, late approvals, and correction journals. These are process signals, not a claim about payroll accuracy. A remote preparer can assemble a journal packet and explain variances. An authorized owner should approve payroll, tax decisions, and payment release.

## Operating boundary
A remote preparer can assemble a journal packet and explain variances. An authorized owner should approve payroll, tax decisions, and payment release. Keep approval, payment release, legal interpretation, and material accounting judgment with designated owners.

## Methodology and limitations
Method: 10 public sources were used to distinguish documentation and control guidance from local employment and tax advice. Confirm jurisdiction-specific requirements independently. Recommendations should be tested against the organization’s systems, contracts, jurisdiction, and risk tolerance.
