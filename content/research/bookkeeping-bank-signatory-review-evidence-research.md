## Review packet
Compare bank records, internal approvals, and current responsibilities. Record discrepancies as exceptions with owners and deadlines.
## Methodology and limitations
Public records, internal-control, security, audit, fraud, labor, and operations guidance informs the process. Bank-specific procedures vary.
