## Key statistic
The research set contains 10 authoritative sources spanning records, controls, accounting standards, access, and fraud risk.

## Count protocol
Use a dated count plan, numbered count sheets, location ownership, and a reconciliation from observed quantities to the inventory system. Record voided sheets and recounts.

## Adjustment evidence
Every adjustment should identify the item, quantity difference, reason, preparer, reviewer, and resulting journal or system change. Exceptions deserve a separate queue.

## Methodology and limitations
This is a workflow framework, not inventory valuation advice. The entity’s policy, system design, physical environment, and accounting owner determine the appropriate treatment.
