## Key statistic
This 10-source research set supports evidence and segregation principles, not a universal reimbursement benchmark.
## Methodology
Translate policy into fields: employee, trip, business purpose, receipt, amount, currency, account, approver, and exception reason. Sample approved and rejected items.
## Control design
Support checks completeness and prepares coding. An independent approver decides policy exceptions; payment release stays with an authorized owner.
## Limitations
Tax deductibility and reimbursement rules vary. Obtain jurisdiction-specific advice before setting policy.
