## Key statistic
This report reviewed 10 public sources; it does not claim that every business can close in one fixed number of days.
## Methodology
We translated control and recordkeeping guidance into a dependency-aware calendar with preparer, reviewer, evidence, and exception fields.
## Measurement
Measure task completion against the agreed cutoff, missing evidence, review returns, and exception aging. Keep definitions stable between cycles.
## Limitations
Calendar length depends on systems, reporting requirements, staffing, and judgment. Calibrate the schedule after two or more cycles.
