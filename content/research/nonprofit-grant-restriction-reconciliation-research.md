## Key statistic
Ten public sources informed this report; it does not estimate nonprofit compliance or grant error rates.

## What the evidence supports
Fund accounting needs clear source records, defined responsibilities, and reconciliations that preserve the reason for each restriction.

## Reconciliation design
Maintain fund, restriction, award, period, activity, balance, evidence, and reviewer fields. Separate data preparation from interpretation and approval.

## Methodology and limitations
The review covered control, recordkeeping, security, fraud, labor, and bookkeeping sources. Grant terms and reporting obligations vary.
