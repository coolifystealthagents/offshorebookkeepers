## Key statistic
Ten authoritative sources were reviewed; no universal review frequency is claimed.

## What the evidence supports
Access should be authorized, appropriate to duties, reviewed, and removed when no longer needed. MFA strengthens authentication but does not replace authorization review.

## Review design
Export the user-role list, have managers attest to need, investigate privileged access, document exceptions, and confirm remediation. Keep the dated evidence set.

## Methodology and limitations
The evidence set includes control, security, fraud, recordkeeping, and labor sources. Cadence should reflect system risk and workforce change.
