## Research question
Travel expense records often span receipts, card feeds, reimbursements, and an employee's explanation. A good bookkeeping record joins those sources without turning a missing detail into an unsupported assumption.

## Evidence pattern
Capture traveler, date, location, business purpose, vendor, amount, currency, payment method, receipt, approval, and reimbursement status. Reconcile corporate-card activity and employee claims to the ledger, then age missing receipts and disputed items.

## Review boundary
Bookkeeping can collect, code, reconcile, and queue exceptions. The finance owner decides policy exceptions, tax treatment, personal-use treatment, and approval of unusual claims.

## Methodology and limitations
IRS expense and recordkeeping guidance plus control, audit, fraud, cybersecurity, and small-business sources inform the evidence model. Deductibility and employer policy depend on facts and jurisdiction.
