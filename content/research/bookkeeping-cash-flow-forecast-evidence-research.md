## Key statistic
This review uses 10 public sources; that is the evidence-set size, not a forecast accuracy claim.
## Evidence model
Use one row per forecast item with source, date, amount, owner, confidence, and next review date. Keep assumptions visibly separate from imported facts.
## Variance workflow
Compare forecast and actual cash by period, classify each variance, and retain the explanation beside the revised assumption. Escalate material or unexplained movements.
## Methodology and limitations
The sources address records, controls, security, and oversight. They do not establish a universal forecast horizon or accuracy threshold.
