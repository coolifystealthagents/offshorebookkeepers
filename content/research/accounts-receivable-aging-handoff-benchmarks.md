## Key statistic
This report reviewed 10 authoritative public sources and separates sourced control principles from operating recommendations. The count is the evidence-set size, not a claim about market performance.

## What the evidence supports
Aging work becomes reliable when every exception has an owner, next action, and evidence trail. Start with the 10-source evidence set and map invoice status, customer contact, dispute reason, promise date, and escalation owner.

## Measurement design
The useful measures are unresolved items by age band, missing dispute evidence, unapplied cash, and handoff completeness. A large contact count can still represent poor control. Preparation can be delegated; credit decisions, write-offs, and customer commitments require the authorized business owner. Use an exception log and weekly review.

## Operating boundary
Preparation can be delegated; credit decisions, write-offs, and customer commitments require the authorized business owner. Use an exception log and weekly review. Keep approval, payment release, legal interpretation, and material accounting judgment with designated owners.

## Methodology and limitations
Method: the sources establish documentation, access, internal-control, audit, and workforce context. They do not establish a single collection-performance benchmark. Recommendations should be tested against the organization’s systems, contracts, jurisdiction, and risk tolerance.
