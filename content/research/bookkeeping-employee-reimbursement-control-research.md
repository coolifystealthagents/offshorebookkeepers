## Key statistic
The framework reviews 10 public references; this is not a claim about reimbursement error rates.
## Evidence packet
Use a consistent packet with claimant, transaction date, merchant, purpose, cost center, receipt, policy result, and approval.
## Exception review
Trend missing receipts, duplicate amounts, weekend activity, split transactions, and repeated policy overrides. Review patterns without assuming misconduct.
## Methodology and limitations
The sources address controls, records, security, auditability, and workforce context. Tax treatment and policy thresholds require local review.
