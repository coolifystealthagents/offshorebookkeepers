## Key statistic
This report reviews 10 authoritative sources and uses that count only to describe the evidence set.

## Rollforward design
A useful schedule shows opening balance, additions, releases, adjustments, closing balance, source document, owner, and review status. Reconcile the closing balance to the ledger.

## Exception review
Flag expired agreements, missing invoices, unusual additions, negative balances, unreconciled releases, and stale schedule lines.

## Methodology and limitations
Accounting classification and amortization depend on the facts and applicable standards. This is an evidence workflow, not accounting advice.
