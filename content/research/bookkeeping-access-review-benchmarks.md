## Key statistic
This report reviewed 10 authoritative public sources and separates sourced control principles from operating recommendations. The count is the evidence-set size, not a claim about market performance.

## What the evidence supports
Access review quality is measured by timely decisions and evidence, not by the number of permissions granted. Map the 10-source evidence set to system inventory, role owner, MFA status, last review, exceptions, and offboarding evidence.

## Measurement design
Track accounts without owners, stale permissions, failed MFA enrollment, overdue reviews, and unresolved offboarding tickets. These are control indicators, not security guarantees. Remote preparers need only the systems and scopes required for assigned work. Client owners retain approval of privileged access, bank permissions, and exceptions.

## Operating boundary
Remote preparers need only the systems and scopes required for assigned work. Client owners retain approval of privileged access, bank permissions, and exceptions. Keep approval, payment release, legal interpretation, and material accounting judgment with designated owners.

## Methodology and limitations
Method: 10 public sources cover security, controls, records, audit, and workforce context. Validate the actual configuration with the organization’s security owner. Recommendations should be tested against the organization’s systems, contracts, jurisdiction, and risk tolerance.
