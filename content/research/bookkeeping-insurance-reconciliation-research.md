## Key statistic
The 10-source evidence set supports control design but does not provide a universal insurance-cost benchmark.
## Methodology
Map each invoice to a policy period, payment, prepaid schedule, monthly release, and renewal or cancellation event. Reconcile the schedule to the ledger.
## Operating controls
Use preparer and reviewer fields, a renewal calendar, documented allocation logic, and an exception log for credits or unexplained changes.
## Limitations
Coverage, accounting treatment, and tax rules vary. This is an operating framework, not insurance or accounting advice.
