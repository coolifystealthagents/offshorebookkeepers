## Research question
Intercompany balances are reliable only when both entities describe the same event the same way. A net balance can conceal timing, currency, coding, or omitted-entry problems.

## Evidence pattern
Reconcile by entity pair and transaction identity. Record confirmations, unmatched rows, currency basis, settlement status, and aging. Keep the original entries visible while documenting any approved elimination or reclassification.

## Review boundary
Bookkeeping can prepare reciprocal matching and chase missing support. The reporting owner decides consolidation scope, eliminations, foreign-exchange treatment, and material unresolved differences.

## Methodology and limitations
The source set combines consolidation, recordkeeping, internal control, audit, and security guidance. It does not determine the reporting model or legal relationship between entities.
