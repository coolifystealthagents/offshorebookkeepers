## Practical guidance

Record processor period, channel, currency, payout reference, pending items, refunds, disputes, and reserves.

## Build the bridge

Start with gross transactions, subtract refunds and fees, identify reserves, and tie net to the bank deposit. Preserve processor references.

## Review timing

Age cross-period payouts and keep dispute or refund ownership clear. Changes to fee, reserve, or foreign-exchange treatment require review.
