## Practical guidance

Start with the source record, date, owner, and amount relevant to vendor statement reconciliation. Record each item separately so timing and classification differences remain visible.

## Keep the evidence connected

 Compare statement invoices, credits, and payments to the payables ledger. Classify each difference as timing, missing document, duplicate, dispute, or unapplied cash.

## Review the exception list

 Retain the statement, comparison, and resolution note together. Vendors with recurring differences need a focused intake check rather than repeated unexplained adjustments.
