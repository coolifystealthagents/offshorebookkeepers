## Fix the monthly source set
Save the statement, transaction export, cardholder list, receipt folder, and prior open-item report. Mark the statement close date and ledger period.
## Match each charge
Compare merchant, date, amount, currency, receipt, business purpose, and account. The [IRS recordkeeping guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/recordkeeping) explains why supporting documents matter for recorded expenses.
## Use a missing-receipt queue
Send a specific request with the transaction, cardholder, deadline, and acceptable evidence. Keep the charge open if the response is incomplete.
## Connect the controls
Use the [bank reconciliation checklist](/blog/bank-reconciliation-support-checklist) and [transaction coding workflow](/blog/daily-transaction-coding-workflow) to preserve review ownership. Do not approve a charge solely because it appears on the statement.
## Review the exceptions
Inspect duplicates, personal charges, foreign currency differences, and aged requests. Review the first two cycles with the finance owner.
