## Identify the governing plan

Retain the plan version, eligible transaction definition, earning event, approval status, and payout timing. If the plan changed, split the population at the effective date.

## Reconcile activity to the source

Compare the commission population to approved sales, invoices, collections, or another stated trigger. Show exclusions and reversals separately; do not infer eligibility from a sales total.

## Track paid and unpaid portions

Tie payouts to bank or payroll evidence and leave unpaid earned amounts visible. Investigate credits, cancellations, and disputed transactions with the plan owner.

## Use a rollforward

Show opening accrual, new earned activity, reversals, payments, adjustments, and ending balance. Include the owner for every open difference and decision.

## Make the handoff reviewable

Keep the final packet with its source links, status, reviewer, and next review date. A clear preparation boundary lets Offshore Bookkeepers support recurring finance work while the business keeps control of approvals and accounting decisions.
