## Capture the policy facts

Record insurer, policy reference, coverage start and end, premium, payment date, and installments or endorsements. Keep the declaration or invoice attached; coverage dates drive the release pattern.

## Build a transparent rollforward

Show opening balance, additions, monthly expense release, credits or cancellations, adjustments, and ending balance. Label partial periods and use a consistent convention.

## Flag changes promptly

Cancellations, renewals, refunds, and coverage changes can make an old schedule stale. Create an exception with the document and effective date, then route it for review.

## Reconcile the ending balance

Compare the schedule to the prepaid ledger and investigate differences by source. List policies ending soon, missing documents, and corrections awaiting approval.

## Make the handoff reviewable

Keep the final packet with its source links, status, reviewer, and next review date. A clear preparation boundary lets Offshore Bookkeepers support recurring finance work while the business keeps control of approvals and accounting decisions.
