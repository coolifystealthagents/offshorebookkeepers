## Classify the balance by source

For each credit, record customer, transaction references, date, amount, and likely cause: unapplied payment, credit memo, duplicate receipt, returned invoice, or correction pending.

## Age the unresolved items

Sort by age and apply the business’s approved review threshold. Older items may require customer correspondence, a missing invoice, or a refund decision; keep outreach with the row.

## Separate research from refund approval

Support can propose possible applications and show evidence. The authorized owner decides whether to apply a credit, issue a refund, or correct an entry. Do not move a credit merely to reduce aging.

## Reconcile after decisions

Link an approved action to the resulting invoice, refund, or journal entry. Re-run the credit-balance list and retain before-and-after evidence.

## Make the handoff reviewable

Keep the final packet with its source links, status, reviewer, and next review date. A clear preparation boundary lets Offshore Bookkeepers support recurring finance work while the business keeps control of approvals and accounting decisions.
