## Practical guidance

Gather invoice, approval, delivery evidence, description, location, custodian, and project reference. Separate project costs from unrelated invoices.

## Present judgment

Thresholds, useful lives, components, and repairs versus improvements may require professional judgment. The bookkeeper prepares facts; the finance owner approves treatment.

## Align and review

Record approved asset fields, reconcile additions and disposals, and check the first depreciation cycle. Preserve the approval reference.
