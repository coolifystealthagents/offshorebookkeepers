## Standardize the request
Use one tracker with request ID, period, account, evidence needed, owner, date sent, deadline, status, reminder, and escalation. Avoid vague requests such as “send support.”
## Explain the evidence rule
State why the item is needed and what an acceptable record looks like. The [IRS recordkeeping guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/recordkeeping) supports retaining documents that substantiate recorded transactions.
## Keep reminders factual
The bookkeeper follows the cadence, records responses, and updates the status. The manager or owner decides whether to accept an alternative or waive a requirement.
## Connect the handoff
Use the [SOP handoff template](/blog/bookkeeping-sop-handoff-template) and [reviewer rules](/blog/bookkeeping-reviewer-handoff-rules) to make the tracker part of the close packet. Preserve the request history.
## Review the queue
Inspect aged requests, repeat requesters, missing periods, and items that block reporting. Improve the source list after two cycles.
