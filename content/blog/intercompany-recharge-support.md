## Practical guidance

Start with the source record, date, owner, and amount relevant to intercompany recharge support. Record each item separately so timing and classification differences remain visible.

## Keep the evidence connected

 Use one recharge reference on both entity ledgers and record the allocation basis, period, and approval. Unmatched counterpart entries should have a named resolver.

## Review the exception list

 At close, confirm each recharge has a counterpart record and that old unmatched balances have an owner and next action.
