## Practical guidance

Start with the source record, date, owner, and amount relevant to journal-entry support index. Record each item separately so timing and classification differences remain visible.

## Keep the evidence connected

 Give each non-routine entry a reference, purpose, period, accounts, evidence, preparer, reviewer, and posting date. Sample the index for unsupported reversals.

## Review the exception list

 Look for missing approvals, duplicate references, unsupported reversals, and entries posted after the review cutoff.
