## Start with the settlement report

Record processor, settlement date, gross activity, refunds, fees, reserves, chargebacks, and net amount. Keep the report version and period; a bank deposit alone cannot explain components.

## Tie the net to the bank

Match the reported net settlement to the deposit, allowing for bank timing. Label differences as pending, split settlement, reserve movement, currency effect, or unresolved.

## Keep fees and refunds distinct

Separate processor fees, customer refunds, chargebacks, and reserve releases. Preserve original transaction or settlement references so the path from sale to cash remains traceable.

## Review aging differences

The exception queue shows settlements not deposited, deposits without reports, and components that do not tie. Review aged items with the processor owner or finance reviewer.

## Make the handoff reviewable

Keep the final packet with its source links, status, reviewer, and next review date. A clear preparation boundary lets Offshore Bookkeepers support recurring finance work while the business keeps control of approvals and accounting decisions.
