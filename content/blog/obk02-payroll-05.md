## Define the payroll boundary

A quality sample for payroll has to test the evidence, not only the count of items that moved. A payroll can look finished while the next reviewer still cannot say who decided, which source was used, or when the case closed, and that gap is exactly what this control is meant to remove. Offshore Bookkeepers documents payroll inside the payroll service, with bank reconciliation and accounts payable as related offshore bookkeeping operations workflows for accounting firm owners and controllers. The expected steps are 'check the source file', 'route the approval', and 'flag the variance', each with a source and a date. the accounting owner reviews the packet before the offshorebookkeepers.com record is closed.

## Connect each item to its source

For payroll, the minimum trail is a dated source, a named reviewer, a stated close condition, and a note on what remains unresolved. At Offshore Bookkeepers, payroll work belongs to the payroll service alongside bank reconciliation and accounts payable. A specialist working with accounting firm owners and controllers records 'check the source file', 'route the approval', and 'flag the variance', then attaches the dated payroll source for the accounting owner. The offshorebookkeepers.com queue shows both the entry date and the reviewer who accepted it.

## Route the owner decision

A prepared payroll packet is not an approval; it is a reviewed question waiting for the person who owns the consequence. Inside Offshore Bookkeepers, payroll follows the same offshore bookkeeping operations pattern as bank reconciliation, accounts payable, and the payroll service itself. The specialist works through 'check the source file', 'route the approval', and 'flag the variance', then dates the source for the accounting owner. The offshorebookkeepers.com reviewer checks the sequence before closure.

## Verify, close, and retest

Each payroll shift should end with a dated handoff that names what moved, what stalled, and who owns the next check. For accounting firm owners and controllers, Offshore Bookkeepers separates preparation from approval across its offshore bookkeeping operations services, including payroll, bank reconciliation, and accounts payable. The specialist completes 'check the source file', 'route the approval', and 'flag the variance' on payroll; the accounting owner decides what leaves the queue. The offshorebookkeepers.com entry shows who prepared and who approved.
