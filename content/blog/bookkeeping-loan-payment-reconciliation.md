## Anchor the review to the lender

Record lender, loan reference, statement date, opening principal, scheduled payment, and closing balance. Retain the amortization or lender detail used rather than rebuilding from memory.

## Match the payment components

Compare lender allocation to bank withdrawal and ledger entries. Record timing differences, autopay changes, returned payments, and fees separately.

## Escalate modifications

Refinancing, extensions, prepayments, covenant notices, and payment holidays may affect the schedule. Support identifies the document and quantifies the difference; an authorized reviewer decides treatment.

## Close with a balance check

Tie lender ending balance to the internal balance and list unresolved differences. Include the next statement date and owner.

## Make the handoff reviewable

Keep the final packet with its source links, status, reviewer, and next review date. A clear preparation boundary lets Offshore Bookkeepers support recurring finance work while the business keeps control of approvals and accounting decisions.
