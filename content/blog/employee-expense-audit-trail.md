## Practical guidance

Capture claimant, date, merchant, amount, currency, purpose, account, receipt status, approver, and reimbursement status.

## Keep approval independent

The bookkeeper checks completeness and coding; a manager or finance owner accepts the business purpose and policy exception.

## Reconcile and sample

Match report to payment and ledger. Sample accounts, employees, amounts, and exceptions while limiting sensitive detail to approved systems.
