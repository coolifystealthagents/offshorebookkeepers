## Identify the asset precisely

Use the asset register identifier, description, location, responsible department, acquisition date, recorded cost, and approval or disposal notice. Similar equipment names are not enough.

## Capture proceeds and condition

Record sale proceeds, trade-in details, scrap evidence, or a statement that no proceeds were received. Keep the evidence and distinguish a disposal from a temporary move or an asset still in use.

## Reconcile the register movement

The bookkeeper can compare the disposal log to the asset register and identify cost, accumulated depreciation, and net-book-value fields requiring review. The authorized reviewer decides the entry and any gain or loss treatment.

## Close with a retained packet

A completed packet includes the asset identity, approval, date, proceeds evidence, register update, journal reference, and unresolved questions. Review missing approvals before the period closes.

## Make the handoff reviewable

Keep the final packet with its source links, status, reviewer, and next review date. A clear preparation boundary lets Offshore Bookkeepers support recurring finance work while the business keeps control of approvals and accounting decisions.
