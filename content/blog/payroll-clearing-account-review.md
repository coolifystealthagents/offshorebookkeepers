## Practical guidance

Start with the source record, date, owner, and amount relevant to payroll clearing account review. Record each item separately so timing and classification differences remain visible.

## Keep the evidence connected

 Match the payroll register, funding entry, deductions, remittances, and bank movement by pay period. A clearing balance should be explained, not merely forced to zero.

## Review the exception list

 Send the reviewer the reconciliation, evidence references, and a dated plan for anything that cannot clear in the current period.
