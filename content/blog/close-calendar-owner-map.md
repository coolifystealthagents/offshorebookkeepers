## Practical guidance

Start with the source record, date, owner, and amount relevant to close calendar owner map. Record each item separately so timing and classification differences remain visible.

## Keep the evidence connected

 List each close task with business day, preparer, reviewer, dependency, evidence, and completion rule. Keep unusual entries and final sign-off with the designated finance owner.

## Review the exception list

 After close, note late tasks and recurring blockers, then adjust the next calendar instead of silently extending every deadline.
