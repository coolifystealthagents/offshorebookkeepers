## Map the contract inputs
Record customer, contract, billing event, service period, amount, currency, schedule, and evidence link. Version the schedule when terms change.
## Validate the source
Compare the schedule to the contract, invoice, receipt, and approved amendments. The [IRS recordkeeping guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/recordkeeping) supports preserving documents that substantiate recorded transactions.
## Flag judgment points
The bookkeeper can update dates, calculate releases, and reconcile totals. The controller, owner, or CPA decides unusual terms and accounting treatment.
## Connect the handoff
Pair the [AR aging guide](/blog/accounts-receivable-aging-handoff) with the [management reporting packet](/blog/management-reporting-packet-bookkeeper). Include open contracts and schedule exceptions in the review packet.
## Review a full cycle
Test a contract from invoice through release, then inspect amendments and cancellations. Keep the reviewer sign-off with the schedule.
