## Practical guidance

Start with the source record, date, owner, and amount relevant to management reporting variance bridge. Record each item separately so timing and classification differences remain visible.

## Keep the evidence connected

 Define the two comparison periods and link each material movement to ledger detail or an operating report. Do not label a change as volume or timing without evidence.

## Review the exception list

 Keep the bridge tied to the report version used in the meeting. If a number changes later, record the changed source and explanation.
