## Practical guidance

Compare normalized names, identifiers, addresses, and approved contacts. Escalate possible matches rather than merging on name alone.

## Protect bank changes

Independent confirmation and authorized approval are required for bank changes. Never combine change approval with related payment release.

## Preserve history

Record merges, affected IDs, dates, and reviewers. Check open credits and invoices before archiving inactive vendors.
