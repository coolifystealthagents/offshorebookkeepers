## Define the support boundary
Write what the bookkeeper collects, codes, reconciles, and reports. State that tax advice, nexus decisions, exemptions, and filing sign-off remain outside the role unless a qualified owner assigns them.
## Build a source checklist
Collect invoices, receipts, customer details, exemption evidence, payment records, and prior filings. The [IRS recordkeeping guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/recordkeeping) explains why records should support income, expenses, and tax-return items.
## Make questions visible
Use a queue for missing location facts, uncertain tax codes, changed exemptions, and unusual transactions. Each row needs a source, owner, deadline, and decision.
## Link the operating workflow
Pair the [transaction coding workflow](/blog/daily-transaction-coding-workflow) with the [AR aging handoff](/blog/accounts-receivable-aging-handoff). Keep the evidence packet separate from a tax opinion and require a reviewer before filing inputs are final.
## Review before delivery
Trace a sample from source to code to report. Have the owner or tax adviser confirm the open questions and preserve the approved filing packet.
