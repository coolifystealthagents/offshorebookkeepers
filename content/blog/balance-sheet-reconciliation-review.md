## Practical guidance

Cash needs statements and reconciliations; payroll liabilities need registers and filings. Define the source, period, and acceptable difference for each account.

## Explain movement

A stable balance can hide an old unreconciled item, while a large movement may be supported by a new contract. Note the driver, source, and whether it should reverse.

## Age open items

Separate timing, missing entries, duplicates, classification questions, and possible errors. Keep the preparer’s work, reviewer question, answer, and disposition together.
