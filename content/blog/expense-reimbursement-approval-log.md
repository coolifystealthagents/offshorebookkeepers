## Practical guidance

Start with the source record, date, owner, and amount relevant to expense reimbursement approval log. Record each item separately so timing and classification differences remain visible.

## Keep the evidence connected

 Record claimant, business purpose, receipt status, approver, amount, and payment reference. A missing receipt and a policy exception require different decisions.

## Review the exception list

 Reconcile approved claims to the payment report and investigate claims paid without an approval reference or approved but still unpaid.
