## Practical guidance

Start with the source record, date, owner, and amount relevant to weekly cash commitments review. Record each item separately so timing and classification differences remain visible.

## Keep the evidence connected

 List approved bills, payroll funding, taxes, debt payments, recurring charges, and unusual commitments by expected date. Mark each item confirmed, estimated, or awaiting approval.

## Review the exception list

 Compare the commitments list with bank activity each week and carry forward only items with a named owner and current expected date.
