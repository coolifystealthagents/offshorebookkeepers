## Define the vendor population

State the period, payment accounts, excluded categories, and source reports used. Include vendors added or changed during the year so the review has a defensible scope.

## Compare identity fields carefully

Check legal name, address, tax-form status, vendor identifier, and duplicate records against the approved source. Do not overwrite a record from a single email; record the evidence.

## Separate payment research from tax advice

Support can identify payment totals and missing or conflicting records. It should not determine reportability or give filing advice; preserve the tax owner’s decision.

## Keep a correction queue

List missing forms, address changes, duplicate vendors, payment mismatches, and unresolved ownership. Give each item an owner and next action.

## Make the handoff reviewable

Keep the final packet with its source links, status, reviewer, and next review date. A clear preparation boundary lets Offshore Bookkeepers support recurring finance work while the business keeps control of approvals and accounting decisions.
