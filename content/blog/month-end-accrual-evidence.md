## Practical guidance

Start with the source record, date, owner, and amount relevant to month-end accrual evidence. Record each item separately so timing and classification differences remain visible.

## Keep the evidence connected

 Capture service period, estimate basis, evidence, preparer, reviewer, and reversal plan. Compare the estimate with the eventual bill and explain the difference.

## Review the exception list

 After posting, compare the accrual with the settled bill and document whether the difference came from timing, estimate error, or changed service.
