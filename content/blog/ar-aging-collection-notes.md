## Practical guidance

State whether aging uses invoice date, due date, or another basis. Keep credits, unapplied cash, disputes, and payment plans visible so one large balance is not mistaken for one problem.

## Write an actionable note

Name the customer, invoice, last verified contact, response, next action, owner, and follow-up date. Avoid vague phrases such as checking or will pay.

## Reconcile first

Compare aging to the ledger and customer detail before collection action. The bookkeeper can prepare a correction; an authorized reviewer approves write-offs and term changes.
