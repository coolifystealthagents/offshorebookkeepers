## Practical guidance

Record vendor, description, frequency, amount pattern, source, approver, and end or renewal date.

## Check change signals

Compare amount, lines, remit-to details, duplicate service, and term. Route changes to the designated approver rather than silently accepting them.

## Reconcile afterward

Confirm account, period, payable status, and recurring end rule. Periodically review dormant subscriptions and outdated contacts.
