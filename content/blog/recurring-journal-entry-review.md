## Practical guidance

List template, accounts, formula, frequency, dates, source schedule, preparer, reviewer, and approval.

## Check evidence

Look for ended services, changed terms, new departments, closed projects, or balances that no longer behave as expected.

## Approve and retire

Record approved changes with reason and effective date. Reconcile after posting and retire obsolete templates deliberately.
