## Practical guidance

State channels, locations, periods, exemptions, credits, and adjustments. Reconcile source sales data to the ledger.

## Keep questions visible

Registration, nexus, taxability, and marketplace treatment may require professional advice. The bookkeeper gathers facts; the tax owner approves treatment.

## Bridge filing and payment

Retain return, confirmation, payment, posting, and any notice or amendment reference. Explain differences and expected clearing dates.
