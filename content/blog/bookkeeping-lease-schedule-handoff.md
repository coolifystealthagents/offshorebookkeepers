## Build the lease file
Record parties, asset, commencement, term, payments, incentives, options, amendments, currency, and evidence links. Give each lease a stable identifier.
## Verify the signed source
Compare the schedule to the agreement and payment history. The [FASB leases resource](https://www.fasb.org/page/PageContent?pageId=/standards/accounting-standards-updates/leases.html) is an authoritative starting point for the applicable accounting standard.
## Route judgment points
The bookkeeper can extract terms and reconcile payments. The controller or CPA approves classification, modifications, and unusual provisions.
## Connect monthly work
Use the [fixed asset handoff](/blog/bookkeeping-fixed-asset-register-handoff) and [prepaid rollforward](/blog/bookkeeping-prepaid-expense-rollforward-workflow) to keep schedules connected to close. Log each amendment before changing the schedule.
## Test one complete lease
Trace an agreement through payment, schedule, entry, and review. Keep the approval and version history with the file.
