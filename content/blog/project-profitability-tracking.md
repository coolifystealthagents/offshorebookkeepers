## Practical guidance

Use one identifier across time, bills, purchase orders, invoices, and revenue schedules. Document shared-cost allocation.

## Separate actuals

Show posted costs and billings apart from forecasts. The bookkeeper prepares bridges; the project or finance owner approves assumptions.

## Reconcile and act

Tie costs to the ledger and invoices to receivables. Assign missing codes, late time, margin movement, and unbilled work to an operational owner.
