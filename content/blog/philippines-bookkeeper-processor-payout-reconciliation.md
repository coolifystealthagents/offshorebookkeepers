## Build the settlement bridge
## Explain one payout difference
If a processor reports $10,000 gross sales, $300 refunds, $120 disputes, $250 fees, and a $9,330 bank deposit, the bridge should show each component, settlement date, reserve or prior adjustment, and source report. A Philippines bookkeeper can match the net to the bank and flag a missing $40; the finance owner decides whether it is a fee, timing item, or processor error.

## Track settlement timing separately
Group transactions by processor settlement date rather than assuming the sales date equals the bank date. Keep an open-item list for payouts initiated but not received, refunds posted after settlement, and reserves released later. Reconcile the next statement to that list and retain the report version used, so the explanation remains reproducible.
For each payout, list gross transactions, refunds, disputes, processor fees, reserves, prior adjustments, and net cash. A Philippines bookkeeper can prepare this bridge and tie the net amount to the bank statement.

## Match by settlement period
Use the processor’s settlement date and the bank’s deposit date as separate fields. A deposit that crosses month-end may be correct timing rather than an error. Preserve both dates so the reviewer can see the cutoff.

## Explain residuals
Residuals should have a category and next action: missing report, unmatched order, delayed refund, reserve movement, duplicate posting, or account mapping question. Do not bury a small difference in a generic fee account without proof.

## Reconcile refunds and disputes
Tie customer refunds and chargebacks to their source transactions and approval records. The bookkeeper records the evidence; the business decides on contested disputes, customer remedies, and any write-off.

## Review the chart mapping
Check whether fees, reserves, refunds, and sales are consistently posted under the written accounting policy. A proposed mapping change should include examples and reviewer approval before it is applied broadly.

## Keep a repeatable close file
Save the settlement report, calculation, bank match, open-item log, and reviewer note together. This makes the work useful to a controller and gives the offshore role a clear recurring handoff.
