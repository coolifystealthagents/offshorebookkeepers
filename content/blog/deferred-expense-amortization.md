## Practical guidance

Record vendor, source, start, end, amount, account, and allocation basis. Make even allocation versus usage-based treatment visible.

## Reconcile the rollforward

Opening balance plus approved additions less release must equal the closing balance and general-ledger account. Investigate differences rather than forcing a plug.

## Review changes

Renewals, cancellations, rebates, and term changes require an owner’s judgment. Close the schedule deliberately when the benefit period ends.
