## Practical guidance

Start with the source record, date, owner, and amount relevant to cash receipt deposit cutoff. Record each item separately so timing and classification differences remain visible.

## Keep the evidence connected

 List deposit date, accounting date, remittance, and customer source. A receipt in the bank without a matching ledger record needs identification before it is treated as an ordinary collection.

## Review the exception list

 Tie the deposit listing to the reconciliation and leave the owner a dated list of unresolved items. Do not erase an in-transit item simply because the bank has cleared.
