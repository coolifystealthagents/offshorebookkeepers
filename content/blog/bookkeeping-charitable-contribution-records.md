## Define the contribution request
Capture recipient, purpose, amount, date, payment method, business connection, and approver. Label whether the request is routine or unusual.
## Preserve the receipt
Keep the receipt, acknowledgment, payment evidence, and approval with the entry. The [IRS charitable contribution guidance](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/charitable-contribution-deductions) explains why documentation matters for deduction questions.
## Keep advice outside the role
The bookkeeper can prepare the packet and apply approved coding. The owner or adviser decides deductibility, limits, and policy exceptions.
## Link the evidence process
Use the [employee reimbursement workflow](/blog/bookkeeping-employee-reimbursement-workflow) and [document retention workflow](/blog/bookkeeping-document-retention-workflow) for consistent evidence handling. Do not label a contribution tax-deductible without approval.
## Review the register
Reconcile approved contributions to payments and the ledger. Inspect missing acknowledgments and unusual recipients before close.
