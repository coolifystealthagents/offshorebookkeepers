## Set the register fields
Use stable fields for the asset ID, description, entity, acquisition and placed-in-service dates, source invoice, project or cost reference, cost, account, location, custodian, status, useful life, method, accumulated depreciation, and reviewer decision. A Philippines bookkeeper can maintain approved data, while proposed classifications and useful-life inputs remain visibly pending until the authorized finance reviewer decides them.

## Start with source records
Match additions to approved invoices and payment or liability records. A vague description or missing approval should place the item on hold, not invite capitalization based on guesswork.

## Review capitalization decisions
The bookkeeper may identify items that appear to meet the documented threshold. The finance owner decides whether an item is a capital asset, expense, lease-related item, or policy exception.

## Trace an asset from purchase to register
For a $7,500 laptop purchase, retain the approved bill, acquisition date, placed-in-service date, asset ID, custodian, location, and proposed useful life. If the bill includes a warranty or accessories, identify what belongs in the asset cost under the client’s policy. The bookkeeper prepares the register row and supporting bridge; the finance owner decides the capitalization and depreciation treatment.

## Reconcile movement, not just totals
At close, compare additions and disposals in the register with the fixed-asset account and related expense. Investigate an asset with no custodian, a disposed item that is still depreciating, or a register balance that changed without a source document. Keep the old row and record the approved correction so the history remains reviewable.

## Reconcile the schedule
Tie cost and accumulated depreciation to the ledger, inspect new additions and fully depreciated items, and explain transfers or disposals. Keep the calculation and comparison in the close packet.

## Control disposals
A disposal needs evidence of what happened, the date, any proceeds, and authorized approval. The support role can prepare the proposed removal and gain-or-loss calculation; the owner approves the accounting result.

## Keep the handoff current
Review the register after purchases, moves, project completions, and each close. A short exception list is more useful than a clean-looking schedule that hides missing custodians or unsupported dates.
