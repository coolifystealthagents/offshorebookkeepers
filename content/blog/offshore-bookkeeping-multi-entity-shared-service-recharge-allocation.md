## Define the outcome before assigning the queue

The bookkeeper applies the documented model and prepares reciprocal entries; management approves the service arrangement and the controller owns policy, tax, and consolidation judgments. Put that boundary in the procedure and in system permissions. A checklist cannot compensate for a user account that allows the preparer to approve, release, or erase the same activity.

## Work through a concrete exception

The schedule uses the version effective for the service month and records the later change for the next allocation rather than silently rerunning history. The point is not to eliminate exceptions. It is to prevent uncertainty from becoming an undocumented entry, an invisible delay, or an approval performed by the wrong person.
