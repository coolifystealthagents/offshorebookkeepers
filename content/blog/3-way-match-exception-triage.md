## Practical guidance

A quantity difference may mean a partial delivery; a price difference may mean an approved change never reached the order. Record the invoice, order, receipt, disputed field, and person who can resolve it before changing an entry.

## Use a hold queue

Statuses such as waiting for receipt, buyer review, vendor correction, and approved variance make the next action clear. Preparation can be offshore; approval and payment release stay with the business.

## Review the pattern

Group exceptions by vendor, category, and cause at close. Repeated quantity issues point to receiving discipline, while repeated price issues may point to outdated purchase orders.
