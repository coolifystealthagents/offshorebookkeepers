## Practical guidance

List platforms, named users, roles, last review, and business owner. Shared credentials weaken traceability.

## Compare to tasks

A reconciliation preparer may not need payment release, payroll approval, vendor administration, or user administration. Remove permissions with no current justification.

## Record changes

Review joiner and leaver actions, privileged access, and temporary exceptions. The sign-off should say what changed, what remains open, and who accepted it.
