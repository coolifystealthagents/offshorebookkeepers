## Practical guidance

For common accounts, say what belongs, what does not, and what source supports it. Use business situations rather than vendor-specific guesses.

## Hold uncertainty

Record missing receipts, proposed category, and answering owner in a visible review status. A hold is safer than a hard-to-find misclassification.

## Sample results

Review by account, value, vendor, and unusual description. Route tax treatment, capitalization, and policy exceptions to the owner, controller, or CPA.
