## Practical guidance

Place statements, payroll, billing cutoffs, and inventory inputs before dependent reconciliations. Reserve time for questions and corrections.

## Define ready

Each line needs preparer, evidence, output, reviewer, due date, and stop condition. ‘Reconcile cash’ is less useful than a testable completion statement.

## Improve a bottleneck

Mark missing-source dependencies and their owners. After close, choose one recurring delay to address in the next cycle.
