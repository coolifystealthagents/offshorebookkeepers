## Write the commission rule
Define the eligible event, rate, cutoff, exclusions, approval, and payment date. Keep the policy version with the calculation.
## Gather source reports
Use approved sales, delivery, customer, and employment reports. The [IRS recordkeeping guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/recordkeeping) explains why source records should support entries and expenses.
## Prepare the draft
The bookkeeper can calculate, reconcile, and document assumptions. The sales or finance owner resolves disputes, exceptions, and policy changes.
## Link the close packet
Connect the [expense accrual handoff](/blog/bookkeeping-expense-accrual-handoff) and [close exception log](/blog/close-exception-log-bookkeeping) so open decisions remain visible. Preserve the original report and approval.
## Review a sample
Trace several rows from source to calculation to entry. Compare the reversal to the eventual payment and update the process where the evidence was unclear.
