## Set the count instructions
Define locations, count date, item identifiers, units, damaged stock treatment, cutoff, and reviewer. Freeze the instructions before counting begins.
## Gather the evidence
Save count sheets, receiving and shipping reports, adjustments, and item master extracts. The [IRS inventory guidance](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p538) describes inventory recordkeeping considerations for businesses.
## Investigate variances
The bookkeeper compares count results to the ledger and lists the difference, likely cause, evidence, and owner. Do not post a material adjustment without approval.
## Connect related controls
Use the [ecommerce reconciliation workflow](/blog/ecommerce-bookkeeping-reconciliation-workflow) and [close exception log](/blog/close-exception-log-bookkeeping) to track cutoff and open differences. Keep count evidence separate from a final adjustment decision.
## Review the handoff
Trace a sample from count sheet to adjustment and ledger. Update the instructions when the same variance repeats.
