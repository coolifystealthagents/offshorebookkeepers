## Fix the reconciliation date
Record the pay period, pay date, payroll provider report date, bank funding date, and ledger period. Date confusion is a common source of unexplained balances.
## Collect the evidence
Keep payroll registers, tax reports, funding confirmations, benefit files, and the journal support together. The [IRS employment tax recordkeeping guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/employment-tax-recordkeeping) describes records employers should retain.
## Prepare and flag
The bookkeeper compares totals, prepares the journal, and lists missing or unusual items. The owner, controller, or payroll specialist decides policy and employee-specific exceptions.
## Use two supporting guides
Pair the [payroll journal controls](/blog/payroll-journal-preparation-controls) with the [reviewer handoff rules](/blog/bookkeeping-reviewer-handoff-rules). Require a second review before a journal or funding instruction is final.
## Test the first cycles
Review the first two payrolls line by line, then check whether liabilities clear when paid. Keep unresolved items open with an owner and due date.
