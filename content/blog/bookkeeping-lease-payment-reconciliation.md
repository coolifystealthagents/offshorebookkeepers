## Start with the agreement

Record the location or asset, lessor, payment frequency, commencement date, renewal or termination terms, and source document. Identify amendments separately so an old schedule is not treated as current.

## Compare expected and actual cash

For each period, show scheduled payment, invoice, bank settlement, fees, credits, and ledger posting. Mark timing differences explicitly. A payment that cleared early may need different follow-up from an amount that does not match.

## Escalate amendments and unusual items

A changed term, rent abatement, security deposit movement, or service component can affect the schedule. Preserve the notice and route it for review; preparation support should not rewrite the schedule alone.

## Retain a period-end summary

The packet should include new or changed agreements, the rollforward, unmatched payments, and decisions needed. This makes recurring lease review auditable.

## Make the handoff reviewable

Keep the final packet with its source links, status, reviewer, and next review date. A clear preparation boundary lets Offshore Bookkeepers support recurring finance work while the business keeps control of approvals and accounting decisions.
