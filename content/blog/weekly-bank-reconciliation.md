## Practical guidance

Define whether the weekly check is interim or full and which accounts are included. Keep the distinction in the close record.

## Match activity

Compare bank activity, ledger, payments, deposits, and transfers. Record statement date and investigate duplicates, reversals, and new payees.

## Age exceptions

Each open item needs first-seen date, amount, explanation, owner, and next action. Keep reconciliation duties separate from vendor and payment administration.
