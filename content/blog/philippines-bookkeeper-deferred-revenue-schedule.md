## Start with the contract trail
Record customer, contract or order reference, invoice, billing date, service period, billed amount, recognized amount, remaining balance, and owner. A stable identifier lets the support role investigate a balance without relying on memory.

## Keep three dates separate
Billing, cash receipt, and revenue recognition can happen on different dates. The schedule should show each one and explain the rule used for the recognition entry so a cutoff difference is not mistaken for missing cash.

## Reconcile movement
Roll opening deferred revenue into additions, releases, credits, refunds, and closing balance. Compare the result to the ledger and investigate unexplained movement by customer or contract.

## Route modifications
An upgrade, cancellation, pause, refund, or new performance obligation may change the accounting treatment. The bookkeeper gathers the amendment and prepares the impact; the finance owner decides the policy application.

## Review exceptions monthly
Focus on negative balances, old items, service periods that ended, invoices without schedules, and schedules that do not agree to the ledger. Make each exception a question with an owner and due date.

## Hand off the recognition file
Include the schedule, movement bridge, ledger tie-out, source links, proposed journals, and open contract questions. This creates useful Philippines-based preparation capacity without transferring final revenue judgment.
