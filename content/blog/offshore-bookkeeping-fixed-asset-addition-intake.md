## Define the fixed asset addition intake boundary

Start with a capital purchase and name the period, entity, currency, source system, preparer, reviewer, and stop rule. The record must show what was reviewed and what was deliberately left outside the lane. A fixed asset addition intake is useful only when another reviewer can reconstruct the same boundary without a private explanation.

## Connect each item to its source

For every item, capture the source identifier, version or date, amount, account, counterparty, and the destination where the result will post. Keep the source value separate from the normalized working value. Do not overwrite an earlier value when a correction arrives. A screenshot may help, but a stable system reference is stronger evidence.

## Route the owner decision

The preparer may collect evidence, apply documented coding, reconcile routine differences, and prepare a decision packet. the fixed asset owner decides whether an item is capital, expensed, or needs specialist review. The preparer must stop when the source conflicts, approval is absent, the amount is material and unsupported, or the item requires a legal, tax, or accounting judgment.

## Verify, close, and retest

After the decision, verify the posted result in the destination system and record who approved it. Reconcile counts back to the queue, retain exceptions with an owner and due date, and retest one ordinary and one adverse case. Close only when the evidence, approval, posting, and reviewer sign-off are all visible.
