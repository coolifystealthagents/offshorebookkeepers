## Practical guidance

Capture account, period, source, type, date found, amount when relevant, owner, reviewer, action, due date, and status.

## Distinguish causes

Use categories such as missing source, timing, duplicate, coding, approval, system, and policy judgment. Set escalation rules for age, reporting impact, and payment risk.

## Close with proof

Resolved means linked correction, source, approval, or conclusion. Review repeated exceptions after close and assign one preventive improvement.
