## Practical guidance

Record sending and receiving entity, date, currency, amount, account, document, and settlement expectation.

## Reconcile directions

Compare receivable to payable and transfers to both bank records. Separate timing, currency, coding, and missing-entry differences.

## Age and approve

Each entity has a preparer and reviewer. Finance approves eliminations and judgments; old differences get corrective actions.
