Published September 7, 2026. This article covers bookkeeping preparation and review support. It is not accounting, tax, legal, payroll, investment, or regulatory advice.

## The problem hiding in the net number

Court fees and outside services can be paid long before the client invoice goes out. A matter-level schedule prevents those costs from aging unnoticed.

A usable legal matter cost reimbursements file identifies the entity, period, currency, source systems, cutoff, preparer, and reviewer. Those details sound routine. They are also what let someone reopen the file next month without relying on memory.

## Save the full population first

Export every in-scope record before filtering. Save the report name, filters, extraction time, row count, and amount total. Compare that population with the prior open-item list and note any account, location, channel, or job that is missing.

Work from the saved source population, then investigate exceptions. Do not edit the original export to make the schedule tie.

## Give the evidence a stable address

Collect the matter ledger, vendor invoice, client authorization, billing record, receipt, and reimbursement schedule. Assign each item a short reference and capture its source, date, amount, currency, status, and owner. Label a missing record as missing. A guess, an estimate, or an unapproved chat screenshot does not replace it.

## Reconcile movement, not just the ending balance

Start with the last reviewed balance or status. Show additions, applications, releases, refunds, transfers, and corrections on separate lines. Tie the result to the current ledger or operating report. Gross movement often exposes two offsetting mistakes that a net total hides.

## Keep an honest exception queue

Use plain labels: missing record, timing difference, amount mismatch, duplicate, stale item, or judgment question. Add an owner, requested action, due date, age, and known financial effect. Close the item only when the stated evidence or approval arrives. If it remains open at cutoff, carry it forward visibly.

## Stop where judgment begins

The preparer can compare observable fields, check arithmetic, and draft a focused question. The preparer should not decide client responsibility and billing treatment. Send that question to the law firm finance owner with the evidence reference, the amount involved, and the response date needed. Save the answer in the workpaper rather than leaving it in a private message.

## Review the awkward items

Reperform large entries, manual adjustments, old exceptions, and records whose status changed. Trace some items from source to schedule and others from schedule back to source. A repeated mismatch may point to a weak written procedure, a stale mapping, or a missing system feed. Record what you observed without guessing at the cause.

## Hand off a file someone else can use

The final packet should include the untouched exports, reconciliation, evidence index, exception queue, approval record, and a short status note. State what tied, what did not, who owns the next action, and which decisions remain open. That is enough for the reviewer to continue the work without reconstructing it.
