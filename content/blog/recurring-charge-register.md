## Practical guidance

Start with the source record, date, owner, and amount relevant to recurring charge register. Record each item separately so timing and classification differences remain visible.

## Keep the evidence connected

 Record vendor, cadence, next expected charge, account, owner, and renewal or cancellation evidence. Compare the register with bank and card activity each close.

## Review the exception list

 Mark services cancelled, paused, replaced, or disputed with dates and supporting evidence so the register remains current.
