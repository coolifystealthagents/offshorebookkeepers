## Practical guidance

Decide whether the pack supports cash planning, margin review, operating decisions, or lender communication. Keep the scope stable.

## Tie figures

Record source, period, reconciliation status, and adjustments. Label estimates and management assumptions separately from posted data.

## Write for a reader

Explain what changed, why, whether it recurs, and what to watch next. Separate evidence from recommendations reserved for management.
