## Practical guidance

Label lines as actual, committed, expected, or scenario. Do not blend a signed bill with an unconfirmed purchase or customer promise.

## Tie dates to evidence

Include date, amount, counterparty, source, confidence, and next confirmation. Connect expected receipts to open invoices and dispute status.

## Flag decisions

The bookkeeper identifies gaps and timing risks. Management decides collection, purchasing, payment timing, or financing responses, then records the assumption.
