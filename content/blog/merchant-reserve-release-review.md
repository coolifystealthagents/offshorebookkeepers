## Practical guidance

Start with the source record, date, owner, and amount relevant to merchant reserve release review. Record each item separately so timing and classification differences remain visible.

## Keep the evidence connected

 Roll forward opening reserve, additions, releases, fees, payouts, and statement support. Review aged or partial releases separately from ordinary merchant receipts.

## Review the exception list

 Retain provider evidence for each movement and review reserves that remain open beyond their expected release date.
