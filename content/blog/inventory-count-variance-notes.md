## Practical guidance

Keep count date, location, item, quantities, counter, recount, and condition note. Explain interruptions or movements during the count.

## Classify cause

Separate timing, unit-of-measure, receiving, shipping, damage, and master-data explanations. Do not invent a root cause when evidence is incomplete.

## Close operationally

The finance reviewer approves material adjustments. Record the corrective action—master-data fix, receiving check, location change, or recount.
