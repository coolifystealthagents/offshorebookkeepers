## Define the reporting scope
Name the client entity, period, jurisdictions, source systems, report cutoff, return preparer, and filing decision owner before assembling the workpaper. A Philippines bookkeeper can prepare within that scope, but missing registrations, disputed taxability, nexus questions, and filing decisions should go to the client’s authorized tax adviser rather than be inferred from transaction data.

## Build a jurisdiction evidence table
For each filing period, list the jurisdiction, transaction source, taxable-sales report, exemption evidence, tax collected, adjustments, cutoff, and filing decision owner. The bookkeeper can reconcile the workpaper to the ledger and flag missing certificates or inconsistent addresses; they should not infer an obligation from a customer location alone.

## Reconcile source to ledger
Compare sales or transaction reports, tax collected, payments, credits, and the sales-tax liability account. Record timing differences and reports that use a different basis from the ledger.

## Keep jurisdiction questions visible
Mark missing addresses, exemption evidence, nexus questions, and unusual transaction types as open items. The bookkeeper assembles facts; the tax owner or adviser decides the treatment.

## Make the handoff reviewable
Tie every amount to a report and extraction date, preserve the calculation, and label estimates or exclusions. If taxable sales differ from the ledger, classify the difference as timing, coding, exemption, or a source gap and route the question. The authorized filer decides the filing position and signs off on the completed packet.

## Tie filing evidence together
Keep the approved return, payment confirmation, supporting reports, and reconciliation together. Do not treat a filed return as proof that the underlying data was complete.

## Review adjustments
Credits, refunds, prior-period corrections, and manual entries need a source, reason, and approval. A support role can propose the reconciliation entry but should not decide a tax position alone.

## Hand off before the deadline
Deliver the workpaper, exceptions, source links, and decision requests with enough lead time for review. This is more useful than a last-minute clean total with no explanation.
