## Practical guidance

List wages, withholdings, benefits, retirement, garnishments, and other liabilities. Assign the report, payment proof, filing, or provider statement for each.

## Bridge and protect

Compare register, journal, payments, and periods. Use approved access and avoid putting full employee data in broad logs.

## Escalate judgment

Late filings, amended payroll, unusual deductions, and eligibility questions go to the responsible payroll or tax owner. Age old balances.
