## Freeze the vendor population
Export vendors, payments, credits, refunds, legal names, addresses, and tax form status for the applicable period. Record the report date.
## Check the source records
Match payment totals to the ledger and vendor file. The [IRS 1099 guidance](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1099-misc) is the authoritative source for current form instructions and filing considerations.
## Log missing information
Track missing forms, name mismatches, duplicate vendors, unusual payments, and address changes. Assign each item to a person with a due date.
## Connect AP controls
Use the [vendor change verification](/blog/vendor-master-change-verification) and [invoice intake workflow](/blog/invoice-intake-bookkeeping-workflow) to trace payment sources. Keep tax advice and filing approval outside the preparation role.
## Review before submission
Have the owner or adviser review the completed report, exceptions, and evidence. Preserve the final approval and submitted data.
