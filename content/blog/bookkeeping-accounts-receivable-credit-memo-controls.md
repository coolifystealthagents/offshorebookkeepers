## Define acceptable reasons
List returns, pricing corrections, service issues, duplicate invoices, and other approved reasons. Require a customer, invoice, amount, date, and owner.
## Match the evidence
Compare the request with the invoice, contract, customer communication, and approval. The [IRS recordkeeping guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/recordkeeping) explains the value of records supporting income and expenses.
## Prepare and route
The bookkeeper can draft the memo, check duplicate risk, and route it. The authorized reviewer decides whether the reason and amount meet policy.
## Reconcile the outcome
Use the [refund reconciliation workflow](/blog/bookkeeping-customer-refund-reconciliation) and [cash application guide](/blog/cash-application-bookkeeping-support) to check the customer balance and any refund or unapplied cash.
## Review aged credits
Inspect old credits, repeated reasons, and memos posted near close. Keep an exception open until the customer balance and source trail agree.
